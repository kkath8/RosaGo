System information

System
RosaGo
Purpose
Context-aware tourism itinerary recommendation system for Santa Rosa, Laguna.
Destination source
RosaGo catalog with City Tourism metadata where available.
Map
OpenStreetMap / Leaflet
Weather
Open-Meteo
Recommendations
Uses destination metadata and user travel preferences. Available geographic information helps recommend places that can be visited more practically together.
AI assistance
Gemini supports natural-language interpretation and explanations. It does not define destination metadata, coordinates, Tourism Type, publication status or weather exposure.

Admin workspace

Dashboard, Destinations, Users, Trips & Itineraries, Catalog Insights and System & Weather provide catalog management and limited operational oversight. Destination writes and operational summaries require the reviewed backend capability. Weather exposure remains curated separately. Account actions and private trip editing are unavailable. Audit Logs provide read-only destination change history.

Provider names describe the system's integrations, not a claim that every service is currently available.
