Admin Help

A quick guide to reviewing RosaGo's catalog and coverage.

Dashboard

Publication totals cover all catalog statuses. Coordinate, Tourism and weather coverage on the Dashboard describe published destinations. These are current counts, not trends.

View Dashboard
Destinations

Search by name, address or category, combine filters, and open a record to inspect its information and provenance. Add Destination creates a Draft. Edit Destination saves shared catalog fields. Publish, move to Draft, archive and restore use a confirmation page; restoration always returns to Draft. Concurrent changes require reloading before retrying.

Review Destinations
Catalog Insights & Needs Attention

Choose Published, All, Draft or Archived to review coverage. Publication Overview always uses the full catalog. Needs Attention lists data-quality flags, not errors. Filter the issue list to review a particular gap.

View Catalog Insights
Users

Search account names or emails in the limited account directory. Passwords and private activity are excluded. Account disabling, password overrides and impersonation are unavailable.

View Users
Trips & Itineraries

Review saved-trip totals and destination usage. Popularity counts distinct saved trips containing a destination, not visits or views. Private itineraries cannot be edited here.

View Trip Oversight
System & Weather

Review the Santa Rosa forecast and destinations whose weather exposure is unknown. A forecast failure does not prevent catalog management. Exposure remains separately curated; missing data is never inferred.

View System & Weather
Publication status
Published
Available through RosaGo's public destination features.
Draft
Not visible to normal RosaGo users.
Archived
Retained but inactive in normal destination results.
Tourism Type & categories
Local
Official Tourism metadata identifies the destination as Local.
Common
Official Tourism metadata identifies the destination as Common.
Unclassified
No Tourism Type has been assigned. Unclassified is not Common.

RosaGo Category and the original Tourism Category are separate fields. Raw hours and asterisks are source values, not verified opening-time guarantees.

Coordinates & geographic coverage

Stored coordinate pairs support map pins, routing and geographic grouping. Missing coordinates limit those location features but do not remove an otherwise eligible recommendation. RosaGo does not invent coordinates.

Public map readiness also requires a published, non-test destination. A stored coordinate pair is not a new claim of independent verification.

Weather exposure
Indoor
Curated evidence describes indoor exposure.
Covered
Curated evidence describes sheltered exposure.
Mixed
Curated evidence describes a mix of exposure conditions.
Outdoor
Curated evidence describes outdoor exposure.
Unknown
Exposure has not been verified. Unknown is not assumed to be Outdoor.

Exposure is read-only curation, not a safety rating or a live weather guarantee. Unavailable curation and Unknown exposure are different states.

Account, appearance & signing out

Account shows your own signed-in identity. Settings changes this browser's Light/Dark appearance. Use the recovery-email flow to change your own password. Log out through the account menu when finished.
